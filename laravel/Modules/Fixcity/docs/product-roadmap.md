## Overview

The Fixcity module is the main application module for the FixCity platform, providing core business logic for city service management.

## Vision

Deliver a comprehensive city management platform that enables municipalities to efficiently manage services, assets, and citizen interactions.

## Current State

- **Status:** Core module under development
- **Maturity:** 50%
- **Key Features:** Service management, citizen portal
