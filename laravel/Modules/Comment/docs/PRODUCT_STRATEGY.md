# Comment Module - Product Strategy

**Module:** Comment  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Last Updated:** March 12, 2026  
**Owner:** Product Team

---

## Executive Summary

The Comment module enables community discussions across the platform, driving engagement and providing valuable user insights while maintaining content quality.

## Strategic Pillars

### Pillar 1: Quality First
Prioritize meaningful discussions over volume.

### Pillar 2: Safety
Protect users from spam and abuse.

### Pillar 3: Engagement
Make commenting rewarding.

### Pillar 4: Insights
Learn from community conversations.

---

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Core (Q1 2026)
- Basic commenting on blog/articles
- Essential moderation

### Phase 2: Growth (Q2-Q3 2026)
- Expand to all content types
- Engagement features

### Phase 3: Intelligence (Q4 2026)
- AI moderation
- Analytics

## Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Spam/abuse** | AI filtering, user reporting |
| **Low engagement** | Gamification, notifications |
| **Moderation burden** | Automated tools, community mods |
